Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to shipping, returns, payments, sizing, and order tracking questions.

Shipping & Delivery

What are the delivery timelines?

Standard delivery across metro cities in India takes 3 to 5 business days. For tier-2 cities and remote locations, delivery takes 5 to 7 business days. Express shipping option is available at checkout.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently, TridentWear ships exclusively within India. We are working on launching international logistics soon. Stay subscribed to our newsletter to receive updates on global shipping availability.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?

We offer a hassle-free 30-day return policy. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with tags intact. Please visit our Returns Portal to submit your return request.

How do I request an exchange?

If you have sizing issues or received a damaged drop, you can request an exchange through your customer portal or contact us via email. Once approved, our courier partner will pick up the package and we'll dispatch your fresh size.

Payments & Orders

What payment methods do you accept?

We support all major payment networks, including Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, and **Cash on Delivery (COD)** with zero extra fees.

How can I track my order?

Once your order is processed and shipped, you will receive a tracking link via SMS and email. You can also track your shipment live on our dedicated Tracking Page using your Order ID.

Still Need Help?

Our support crew is available Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM - 7:00 PM IST.
