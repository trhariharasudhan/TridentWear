FAQ

How does AI Fit Check work?

Fit Check uses your camera and an on-device pose-detection model to find your shoulders and hips, then places the garment on you in real time. The video is processed inside your browser and is never uploaded.

How do your sizes run?

Oversized tees are cut roomy by design — size down for a regular fit. Shirts and classic tees are true to size. Every product page has a size guide and a size finder.

How long does delivery take?

Orders ship in 1–2 business days. Metro cities usually receive them in 3–5 days, the rest of India in 5–8 days.

Is shipping free?

Yes — shipping is free on every order across India.

Can I exchange or return?

You can request a size exchange or return within 7 days of delivery for unworn items with tags attached. See our returns policy for details.

Which payment methods do you accept?

UPI, credit and debit cards, netbanking and wallets through Razorpay, plus Cash on Delivery.

How do I track my order?

Use the Track order page with your order ID (it starts with TRD-), or open the order from your account.
