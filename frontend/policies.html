Policies

Draft policies for pre-launch. Have them reviewed before going live.

Shipping

  • Free shipping on all orders within India.
  • Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days.
  • Typical delivery: 3–5 days for metro cities, 5–8 days elsewhere.
  • You'll get a tracking ID once your order ships.

Returns & exchanges

  • Exchange or return within 7 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and have original tags.
  • Refunds for prepaid orders go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of receiving the return. COD refunds are made by bank transfer/UPI.
  • Sale items and items marked final sale cannot be returned.

Privacy

We collect the information needed to process your order (name, contact details, address) and to run your account. Payments are processed by Razorpay; we never see or store your card details. AI Fit Check camera frames are processed on your device and are not uploaded. The optional HD AI render sends the photo you choose to our try-on provider to generate the image and is not stored by us.

Terms

By placing an order you agree to provide accurate information and to pay the amount shown at checkout. Prices are in INR and inclusive of applicable taxes. We may cancel orders in case of pricing errors or stock issues, with a full refund.
