Two friends, one rail of tees and shirts

TridentWear is run by Hari Hara Sudhan and Jeevan Raja. We were tired of tees that lose their shape after three washes and shirts that never quite fit, so we decided to make our own: just T-shirts and shirts, made properly and cut clean.

How it started

  1. The idea

    Two friends, one shared complaint about basics that don't last. We sketched the Trident mark and made a list of exactly two products.

  2. The samples

    Fabric first: 240 GSM combed cotton for tees, oxford, poplin, linen and twill for shirts, bio-washed and pre-shrunk.

  3. The store

    We built this site ourselves, including AI Fit Check, so you can see a piece on you before you order.

  4. Drop 01

    Our first rail of tees and shirts. Small runs, made in India, and a lot of care in every one.
