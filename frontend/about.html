Built from the basics up

TridentWear started with a simple frustration: tees that lose shape after three washes and shirts that never quite fit. So we're starting with just two things — T-shirts and shirts — and making them properly.

01 · Fabric first

Heavyweight, honest cotton

02 · Made in India

Cut and sewn locally

Produced with partner workshops in India, in small runs, so every drop gets the attention it needs.

03 · Try before you buy

AI Fit Check

See a piece on your body with your phone camera before ordering — fewer returns, better fits.
